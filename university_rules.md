# Northbridge University: Undergraduate Academic Regulations

Version: 2026-1. This is a fictional document created for teaching purposes.

## 1. Enrollment and Registration
Rule 1.1: Students must register for courses during the official registration period published in the academic calendar.
Rule 1.2: Late registration is allowed only during the first week of the semester and requires a late registration fee of 50 USD.
Rule 1.3: Students who do not register for two consecutive semesters without an approved leave of absence lose their student status and must apply for readmission.
Rule 1.4: Students may add or drop courses during the first two weeks of the semester, and these changes do not appear on the transcript.

## 2. Course Load
Rule 2.1: Full-time undergraduate students must take between 12 and 20 credits per semester.
Rule 2.2: Students with a semester GPA of 3.5 or higher may request permission to take up to 24 credits in the following semester.
Rule 2.3: Students on academic probation may not take more than 14 credits per semester.
Rule 2.4: The maximum course load in the summer term is 8 credits.

## 3. Attendance
Rule 3.1: Students must attend at least 80% of the scheduled class sessions in each course.
Rule 3.2: A student who misses more than 20% of the sessions of a course without an approved excuse receives a grade of F in that course.
Rule 3.3: Absences caused by documented illness, family emergencies, or official university activities may be excused. Supporting documents must be submitted to the Student Affairs Office within 7 days of returning to class.
Rule 3.4: An excused absence does not remove course requirements. The instructor decides how the missed work can be completed.
Rule 3.5: Arriving more than 15 minutes late may be counted as an absence at the instructor's discretion.

## 4. Examinations
Rule 4.1: Each course has a midterm exam and a final exam unless the course syllabus states a different assessment method.
Rule 4.2: Students must bring a valid student ID card to every exam.
Rule 4.3: Students who arrive more than 30 minutes after an exam has started are not allowed to enter the exam room.
Rule 4.4: A student who misses a final exam because of a documented emergency may request a make-up exam within 5 working days. Make-up exams must be approved by the department head.
Rule 4.5: Mobile phones, smartwatches, and all other electronic devices must be switched off and placed away from the desk during exams.
Rule 4.6: Final exam results are published within 10 working days after the exam date.

## 5. Grading and GPA
Rule 5.1: The university uses the following grades and grade points: A = 4.0, B = 3.0, C = 2.0, D = 1.0, and F = 0.0.
Rule 5.2: The minimum passing grade for a general course is D. The minimum passing grade for a course in the student's major is C.
Rule 5.3: The GPA is calculated by multiplying each course grade point by the course credits, adding the results, and dividing the total by the number of attempted credits.
Rule 5.4: Students may retake a failed course. Only the most recent grade is used in the GPA, but both attempts remain on the transcript.
Rule 5.5: A single course may be retaken at most two times.
Rule 5.6: An Incomplete grade (I) may be given if a student has completed at least 70% of the course work but cannot finish for documented reasons. The course must be completed by the end of the next semester, or the grade becomes F.

## 6. Academic Standing and Probation
Rule 6.1: A student is in good academic standing when the cumulative GPA is 2.0 or higher.
Rule 6.2: A student is placed on academic probation when the semester GPA is below 2.0.
Rule 6.3: Students on probation must meet their academic advisor at least twice during the probation semester.
Rule 6.4: A student who is on probation for three consecutive semesters is dismissed from the university. A dismissed student may submit one appeal.
Rule 6.5: Probation is removed when the cumulative GPA returns to 2.0 or higher.

## 7. Assignments and Late Submission
Rule 7.1: Assignments must be submitted through the official learning platform before the published deadline.
Rule 7.2: Late submissions lose 10% of the total possible score for each day late, for up to three days.
Rule 7.3: Submissions that are more than three days late receive a score of zero unless the instructor has approved an extension.
Rule 7.4: Extension requests must be sent to the instructor before the deadline, except in documented emergencies.
Rule 7.5: In group assignments, every member must be able to explain the submitted work. Instructors may give different grades to different group members.

## 8. Academic Integrity
Rule 8.1: Academic integrity means that all submitted work honestly represents the student's own effort and understanding. Every student is expected to know and follow these rules.
Rule 8.2: Plagiarism is presenting another person's words, ideas, code, or data as your own without proper citation. This includes copying from websites, books, other students, or previous submissions.
Rule 8.3: Cheating includes using unauthorized materials during an exam, copying answers from another student, sharing exam questions, or communicating with others during an exam.
Rule 8.4: Fabrication means inventing or changing data, results, sources, or citations in any academic work.
Rule 8.5: Contract cheating means asking or paying another person or service to complete academic work on the student's behalf. Impersonation means taking an exam for another student or allowing another person to take an exam for you.
Rule 8.6: Artificial intelligence tools, such as chatbots and code generators, may be used only when the course syllabus allows them. When AI tools are used, students must clearly state how they were used, and students remain fully responsible for the correctness of the submitted work.
Rule 8.7: The penalty for a first academic integrity violation is a score of zero on the related work and a written warning. The penalty for a second violation is a grade of F in the course.
Rule 8.8: A third violation, or a serious violation such as impersonation in an exam, can lead to suspension for one semester or dismissal from the university.
Rule 8.9: The instructor must report a suspected violation to the Academic Integrity Committee within 10 working days. The student is informed in writing and may submit a written response within 7 days. The committee announces its decision within 30 days.
Rule 8.10: Records of academic integrity violations are kept by the university for 5 years.

## 9. Leave of Absence and Withdrawal
Rule 9.1: Students may take a leave of absence for a maximum of two semesters in total during their studies.
Rule 9.2: Leave requests must be submitted before the semester starts, except in medical emergencies.
Rule 9.3: Approved leave semesters do not count toward the maximum study duration.
Rule 9.4: Students may withdraw from a single course until the end of week 10 of the semester. The course receives a grade of W, which does not affect the GPA.
Rule 9.5: Tuition is refunded 100% if a student withdraws before the semester starts, 50% if the student withdraws within the first two weeks, and not refunded after that.

## 10. Graduation Requirements
Rule 10.1: A bachelor's degree requires the completion of at least 130 credits.
Rule 10.2: To graduate, students need a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0 and a GPA of at least 2.25 in the courses of their major.
Rule 10.3: The maximum study duration for a bachelor's degree is 6 years (12 semesters).
Rule 10.4: Students must complete a final project or an internship, as required by their program.
Rule 10.5: Students graduate with honors if their cumulative GPA is 3.5 or higher and they have no academic integrity violations.

## 11. Grade Appeals
Rule 11.1: Students may appeal a final grade within 7 working days after the grade is published.
Rule 11.2: The appeal must first be discussed with the instructor. If it is not resolved, the student may send a written appeal to the department head within 5 working days.
Rule 11.3: The decision of the department head is final, except in cases of procedural error, which may be reviewed by the Faculty Academic Council.
Rule 11.4: An appeal can result in a higher grade, an unchanged grade, or a lower grade.
